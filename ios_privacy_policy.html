Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy explains how MV Master - Music Video Maker (the “App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles your information when you use our app. MV Master lets you select your own photos and turn them into music videos using pre-built templates. We have written this policy in plain language because we want you to understand exactly how your information is handled.

Your privacy is important to us. MV Master is designed to work primarily on your device, and we deliberately collect as little information as possible.

FACE DATA

MV Master does not collect, access, use, store, share, or retain any face data or biometric data of any kind.

  • The App does not include facial recognition, face detection, face tracking, face mapping, or any biometric identification technology.
  • When you add a photo to a template, the App simply places your photo into the template; it does not detect, analyze, or extract faces or any facial features from it.
  • No face data or biometric identifiers are created, collected, or derived by the App.
  • Because no face data is collected, none is ever transmitted to us or to any third party, none is stored on our servers, and there is nothing to retain or delete.

PHOTOS AND VIDEOS

  • You choose which photos to use by selecting them from your device’s photo gallery. The App only accesses the photos you specifically select.
  • Your selected photos and the videos you create are processed on your device to produce your music video.
  • We do not upload, store, or keep your photos, videos, faces, or any biometric data on our servers. Your media stays on your device unless you choose to save or share it yourself.
  • When you export, save, or share a finished video, it is handled by your device and the app or service you choose to share it with.

INFORMATION YOU GIVE US

If you contact our support team, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and the contents of your message, so that we can respond to and resolve your request.

DEVICE AND DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION

Like most mobile apps, we may collect limited technical information to keep the App working reliably and to improve it, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and anonymous crash or performance diagnostics. This information does not identify you personally and is not linked to your photos or videos.

ADVERTISING AND ANALYTICS

The App displays advertising using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To serve ads, AdMob may collect and process standard mobile identifiers such as advertising IDs (Apple’s IDFA or Google’s Advertising ID), device type, approximate (non-precise) location derived from your IP address, and general app-usage data. These identifiers are randomly generated and can be reset or limited at any time from your device settings:

  • Apple iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads.

How Google collects and uses this data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services” (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Google AdMob never receives your face data, biometric data, or the photos and videos you create, because those are processed only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

PERMISSIONS WE REQUEST

  • Photo Library: required so you can choose the photos you want to turn into a video. We only access the photos you select.
  • Save to Gallery / Storage: required only when you choose to save a finished video back to your device.

You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. If you do, some features may stop working.

DATA RETENTION

Because your photos, videos, faces, and biometric data are never uploaded to or stored on our servers, we have nothing of that nature to retain or delete. Any limited diagnostic or support information is kept only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected and as permitted by law.

CHILDREN

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us at the email below and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

SECURITY

We take reasonable physical, electronic, and procedural measures to protect the limited information we handle. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

YOUR RIGHTS

You remain in control of your information. You can manage app permissions from your device settings, and you can contact us at any time using the details below to ask questions about this policy or to request that any support information you provided be deleted.

SUBSCRIPTIONS

If you purchase a subscription or premium features, payment is handled by Apple through your App Store account and is subject to Apple’s terms. Please see our Terms and Conditions for details on billing, renewals, and refunds.
