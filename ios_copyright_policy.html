Filing a Copyright Infringement Claim

A legitimate copyright infringement claim should encompass the following details:

  1. The signature, whether digital or physical, of the copyright proprietor or an individual authorized to act on the proprietor’s behalf.
  2. Clear identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed upon.
  3. A detailed description of the location of the allegedly infringing material within the MV Master App or its services, including a URL for precise identification, if possible.
  4. Full contact details of the claimant, including name, physical address, phone number, and email address.
  5. A declaration that there is a bona fide belief that the material in question is used in a manner not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, asserting that the provided information is accurate and that the complainant is authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly being infringed.

Claim Review Process

Upon receipt, our team will promptly review your claim. Should our response indicate that the report does not breach our policies, you may challenge our decision by submitting a formal request via an official government or law enforcement email address. This request should clearly outline the local laws allegedly violated by the content and include any court orders or supporting documents. We commit to addressing these requests as quickly as possible.

Cautionary Note

Both copyright claimants and alleged infringers are urged to carefully consider the implications of filing a copyright infringement notification or counter-notice. If there is any uncertainty regarding your position as the rights holder or your authority to act on behalf of the rights holder, it is crucial to reconsider your submission. False, fraudulent, or bad-faith claims can result in legal and financial repercussions. Ensure you are the rightful owner or have a solid belief that a mistake has been made regarding the removal of the content, and be fully aware of the consequences of making a false claim.
